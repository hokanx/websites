# STAMP BURGER — research fact sheet

Every fact below came from research. Nothing here is invented. Anything I could
not verify is listed under "Open gaps" and must NOT appear on the site until the
client supplies it.

Research constraint: `WebFetch` returned HTTP 403 on every host in this
environment and direct `curl` is blocked by the network policy, so all facts
below were extracted from web-search result snippets rather than full page
scrapes. That limits depth — in particular it cost us reviewer names and the
Instagram photo library.

## Real review quotes (Foursquare tips)

Used verbatim on the site, labeled "Foursquare tip". **No reviewer names were
retrievable**, so the site attributes them to the platform, not to invented
people.

1. "Very yummy, brand new burger joint. I tried their stamp angus burger and the Fries 10/10"
2. "Excellent bun, angus burger was delicious, but fries was oily"
3. "It was so delicious, the chkn frise, buffalo chkn, crispy chkn burger top notch. Small place, super friendly staff"

Quotes 1 and 3 name STAMP items explicitly ("stamp angus burger", "brand new
burger joint"), which is what ties them to this venue rather than to a
neighbouring listing in the same Foursquare city list. Two further tips that
surfaced in the same search ("truffle beef burger", "mushroom burger", "fuji
fries") mention items that appear nowhere on STAMP's menu across three delivery
platforms, so they were treated as bleed from adjacent listings and **discarded**.
